A group of 7 Minecraft players embark on a modded journey to defeat the Wither. At the same time, another group of 7 players begin a similar journey, but instead seek to slay the Netherite Monstrosity. All seems normal with these two groups until, suddenly, a player from each group swaps with each other and is teleported to the other group's world. Can these two groups survive the threats of their natural worlds and achieve their goals while constantly swapping players with one another?
