Team Scallop and friends find themselves in another game of _Minecraft_ where each of them are forced to kill somebody they're assigned in order to win. Everything seems as it was previously, until suddenly, Whisk, the gamemaster of the previous killing game, is dethroned and is forced to participate in the game himself. In his place, Slenderman (Wack) takes the throne. Can the cast achieve their individual goals while surviving the trials and tribulations imposed by Slendy?
