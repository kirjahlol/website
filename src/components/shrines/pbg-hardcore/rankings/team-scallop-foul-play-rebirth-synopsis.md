Team Scallop and friends find themselves in another game where they are forced to kill each other in order to win. Everything seems as it was previously, until suddenly, Whisk, the gamemaster of the previous killing game, is dethroned and is forced to participate in the killing game himself. In his place, Slenderman (Wack) takes the throne. Can the cast achieve their goals while surviving the trials and tribulations imposed by Slendy?
