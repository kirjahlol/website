### How I Found Hardcore

The way I found the Hardcore series is pretty odd. Let's go back to the year 2017 when I was 7, possibly 6 years old. My sister and I used to play this Wii game called _Wipeout: The Game_ a lot as kids. The ragdoll physics in that game were incredibly funny, and we'd always die laughing whenever we played it. Since I enjoyed the game so much, I decided to look it up on YouTube and see if anybody had made content on it, and people had indeed done so. The first result was "[RAGDOLL PHYSICS! - Wipeout (Part 1)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n-4ZcXszAk8)" by [PBGGameplay](https://www.youtube.com/@PBGGameplay), and I clicked on it. Even though I had a hard time remembering who was who, I quickly fell in love with the humuor of the two dudes running the show, "PB" and "Jeff."<sup>\*</sup>

As one does when they enjoy someone's content, I clicked on their channel, and the featured video was part 1 of Minecraft #5.<sup>\*</sup> Curious as to what this series was, I clicked on it. I ended up really enjoying the first part of the series and watched the whole thing over the next few days.<sup>\*</sup> The rest is pretty much history. I watched [Terraria #3](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLr6uCN6eqr18K2OtmtStTuAu6IcaI-acj) as it was coming out, along with [MineZ #2](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLr6uCN6eqr1_EHXeH-1CiZm0ORtpl2oZP) and [Minecraft #6](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLr6uCN6eqr18DIPbFAC2ZwIDXld3-jfn4). I actually didn't fully finish [Starbound](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLr6uCN6eqr18CRFGUABOHDj0RsWllBiSU) despite being there for its release because, respectfully, that season was a snoozefest. I was, of course, also there for the return of the Hardcore series and the release of NB Minecraft #1 in 2021. Despite that season being pretty unpopular in the Hardcore community, I actually enjoyed it, though I'd probably fall asleep watching it now.

### Why I Love Hardcore

To keep it brief, I love hardcore because of the endless hours of entertainment it's provided me with and the wonderful community of people it introduced me to (more on that on the [Community](/shrines/pbg-hardcore/community) page). If I never found Hardcore, I genuinely think I'd be a very different person, and I'll never not be grateful for being able to find this series.
